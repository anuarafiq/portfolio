# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Mixed audience: recruiters and hiring managers skimming fast, design-minded
engineers who inspect technique, and potential clients judging polish. The
site must read quickly, but reward anyone who looks closer.

### Brand Personality
Considered, literate, precise. The site is a personal portfolio presented as
ink on paper - an editorial broadsheet, not a SaaS landing page. Unexpected
moments should feel like fine bookbinding: craft you notice in the hand, never
a gimmick.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Ink-on-paper editorial. Warm cream paper, near-black ink, vermillion/rust
  accent used like a correction stamp.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond (serif display + body) paired with IBM Plex Mono
  (labels, dates, tags). Sharp corners everywhere - no border radius.
- Dark mode is "Charcoal Broadsheet": warm charcoal paper, off-white ink.
- Anti-references: SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, card grids, neon-on-dark.

### Design Principles
1. One signature moment, quiet everywhere else. Motion concentrates where it
   lands hardest (projects -> detail morph); the rest stays still.
2. The page is one continuous surface. Navigation should feel like turning to
   a different part of the same broadsheet, not loading a new app screen.
3. Typography does the work. Hierarchy comes from size, weight, and the
   serif/mono contrast - not color, boxes, or decoration.
4. Everything degrades gracefully. Effects are progressive enhancements;
   without them the site is still complete. prefers-reduced-motion always
   gets a calm, static experience.
