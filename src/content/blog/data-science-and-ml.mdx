### What's Actually Clicking

Feature engineering. The part where you take raw data and turn it into something a model can use. That's where domain knowledge matters more than algorithm choice. A better feature beats a fancier model almost every time.

Pandas. It clicked slowly, then all at once. Now I find myself thinking in DataFrames.

The feedback loop of training, evaluating, and iterating. Once that rhythm makes sense, the process feels less like guesswork.

### What Still Confuses Me

How to pick the right model without just trying all of them. The theory says decision boundaries and assumptions — in practice it often feels empirical.

Hyperparameter tuning. I understand what it is. I don't always understand why certain values work better than others.

Interpreting results honestly. It's easy to report accuracy. Harder to know if the model is actually useful or just fitting your training data well.
